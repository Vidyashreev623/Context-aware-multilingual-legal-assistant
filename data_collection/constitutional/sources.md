# Constitutional Law — Sources

## Primary legislation
1. **The Constitution of India** — India Code — https://www.indiacode.nic.in/ — constitution — India (Central)
2. Constitutional Amendment Acts (1st through most recent) — India Code — https://www.indiacode.nic.in/ — amendment — India (Central)

## Case law / judgments
3. Supreme Court constitutional-bench judgments — https://main.sci.gov.in/judgments — case — India (Supreme Court)
4. High Court judgments on constitutional matters (writ jurisdiction, Article 226) — https://judgments.ecourts.gov.in/ — case — India (State)

## Notes
- Landmark constitutional-bench rulings (e.g. basic structure doctrine, fundamental rights cases) should be tagged `bench_type: constitution_bench` where identifiable from the judgment metadata, since these carry different precedential weight than division-bench rulings.
