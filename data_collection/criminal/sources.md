## Notes
- IPC/CrPC/Evidence Act were substantively replaced by BNS/BNSS/BSA effective 1 July 2024. Both old and new codes are collected because (a) case law up to the transition cites the old codes, and (b) sections have been renumbered, not simply relabeled — the mapping must be preserved in metadata (`superseded_by` / `supersedes` fields), never assumed 1:1.
